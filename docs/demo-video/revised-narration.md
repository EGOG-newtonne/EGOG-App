# EGOG demo video v2 narration and captions

This script avoids claims of third-party verification, real-time data, free
gas, or the absence of a wallet. The `Demonstration Data` label must remain
visible whenever project data is shown.

| Time | Narration | Short on-screen caption |

## Prohibited substitutions

- Do not replace `versioned Snapshot` with `tamper-proof data`.
- Do not replace `gas is sponsored for the user` with `zero gas fees`.
- Do not replace `embedded wallet` with `no wallet needed`.
- Do not describe the Demonstration Data as verified, issued, live, or real-time.
- Do not narrate Registry registration, third-party verification, or credit
  issuance as complete.
