## Motion direction

- Use restrained spatial pushes and spotlight zooms that clarify the workflow.
- Keep Production screenshots large and legible; never stack decorative cards
  over important UI.
- Use EGOG green `#006c49`, accent `#10b981`, ink `#18201d`, and the
  Hanken Grotesk/system-sans hierarchy.
- No background music. This avoids licensing ambiguity and keeps the captioned
  submission understandable without audio.
- Transaction hashes, wallet addresses, and CIDs may appear only where they are
  already public; Google account information must never appear.
